3. Turning Zero-Thickness Surfaces into Watertight 3D Prints (shellThickness)

Here is a classic trap when exporting an implicit surface f(x,y,z)=0f(x, y, z) = 0f(x,y,z)=0 for 3D printing:

  • If you run Marching Cubes directly on f(x,y,z)=0f(x, y, z) = 0f(x,y,z)=0 inside [−B,B]3[-B, B]^3[−B,B]3, the resulting mesh has zero thickness and open boundary edges where the surface hits the bounding box walls. Every 3D printer slicer rejects open, non-manifold sheets!
